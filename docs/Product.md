# A short film before a social network

StageFold serves a person who can write a two-character exchange but does not want to draw every frame or learn a general animation tool. The first job is to turn that exchange into a 20–60 second captioned scene, move the actors and export an actual playable artifact. No microphone or voice is promised.

Excel and a notebook can hold dialogue. They do not compose and render actor motion, timed speech and continuous scene transitions. StageFold must produce that artifact before asking for an account. Return use and willingness to pay remain hypotheses: an author may improve recurring characters, share an alternate ending, or work with a collaborator. A public fork is useful only when its changed shots and source version remain inspectable.

## Observed competition, 24 October 2026

[Pixton](https://www.pixton.com/) already offers comics, storyboards and books without drawing. [Storyboard That](https://help.storyboardthat.com/premium-account/premium-vs-free-account) documents an art library, revisions, audio and image uploads, privacy and print/download features. [Jitter](https://jitter.video/) is a broader browser motion-design service. These observations establish competition, not unmet demand or global novelty.

The chosen scope is a deliberately limited original cast with continuous actor movement, readable timed dialogue and a real export, connected to version-pinned public remixes and role-controlled shared production. It is not a collection of new names for panels, feeds or note-taking tools.

## First-job acceptance

1. Open an explicitly labelled original starter or an empty local film without an account.
2. Edit dialogue, actor start/end positions, gesture and timing; playback must show these changes.
3. Export and reopen the same canonical JSON; export a standalone HTML player that works without the service. Record WebM only if the browser reports a supported MediaRecorder codec, never pretend that HTML is a video file.
4. Create an account and shared project. A second account joins as editor and saves a real revision. A stale save must fail without overwriting work. A viewer cannot write.
5. Owner publishes an immutable cut with explicit reuse permission. A different author forks that exact cut, edits it and publishes an attributed descendant; comparison identifies changes.
6. Remove access, withdraw public visibility, and report abuse through actual server-controlled state transitions. Existing exported artifacts cannot be recalled.

An optional payment adapter must be disabled without all merchant readiness pieces. No fabricated paid state, customer stories, audience or expected income is used.
